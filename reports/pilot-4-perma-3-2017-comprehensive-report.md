# Pilot 4 Comprehensive Research Report: PERMA No. 3 Tahun 2017

> **Status:** `LIVE_PILOT_4_ACTIVE` (Level 1–2 Institutional Baseline Verified; strict UU PDP 27/2022 & SK KMA 2-144/2022 zero-PII protection; not an official Mahkamah Agung RI publication).

## 1. Research Background
Evaluates institutional implementation, gender-sensitive courtroom safeguards, and disability/victim-support gaps under PERMA No. 3 Tahun 2017.

## 2. Regulatory Profile
- **Primary Instrument**: [PERMA No. 3 Tahun 2017 tentang Pedoman Mengadili Perkara Perempuan Berhadapan dengan Hukum](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/209695/perma-no-3-tahun-2017) (enacted 11 July 2017, effective 4 August 2017, BN.2017/No.1084).
- **Judicial Training & Reference**: [Kepaniteraan MA RI Guidelines](https://kepaniteraan.mahkamahagung.go.id/images/artikel/pmppbdh.pdf) and [Ditjen Badilag Training Materials](https://badilag.mahkamahagung.go.id/seputar-ditjen-badilag/inilah-materi-pelatihan-perma-nomor-3-tahun-2017).

## 3. Public Issue Map
- `TAX-05` (Vulnerable Group & Gender Protection): Judicial gender sensitivity, victim intimidation prevention, disability interpreters, and recovery rights.
- `TAX-06` (Victim Restitution & Enforcement): Post-divorce *mut'ah*, *iddah*, and child maintenance (*nafkah anak*) enforcement.

## 4. Discourse Analysis & Ethics Guardrail
Due to high sensitivity under **UU No. 27 Tahun 2022** and **SK KMA 2-144/2022**, Pilot 4 excludes unredacted social-media case narratives and relies on institutional Level 1–2 evidence until trauma-informed, consent-gated `GATE-03` consultations are approved.

## 5. Stakeholder Perspective Report
Covers judges, victim companions (*pendamping PBH*), Komnas Perempuan, LBH APIK, and disability-rights advocates.

Logged in `validated-evidence/pilot4_live_official_records.csv`:
- **5-Province SPPT-PKKTP Audit** ([Komnas Perempuan](https://komnasperempuan.go.id/siaran-pers-detail/siaran-pers-peluncuran-hasil-kajian-penerapan-peraturan-mahkamah-agung-ri-nomor-3-tahun-2017-tentang-pedoman-mengadili-perkara-perempuan-berhadapan-dengan-hukum-di-5-mitra-wilayah-sppt-pkktp-15-desember-2021) & [IJRS](https://ijrs.or.id/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Kajian-Atas-Penerapan-PERMA-3-2017-di-5-Mitra-Wilayah-SPPT-PKKTP-Kepulauan-Riau-Kalimantan-Tengah-DKI-Jakarta-Jawa-Tengah-dan-Maluku.pdf)) across Kepulauan Riau, Kalimantan Tengah, DKI Jakarta, Jawa Tengah, and Maluku.
- **Religious Court Study** ([UMSU Jurnal Ey, 2025–2026](https://jurnal.umsu.ac.id/index.php/ey/article/view/29008)).

## 7. Implementation Gap Analysis
1. Insufficient judicial and law-enforcement socialisation on women's recovery rights and gender power imbalances.
2. Limited budget and infrastructure for psychologists, sign-language/disability interpreters, and victim accompaniment.
3. Cross-agency mis-coordination within SPPT-PKKTP criminal-justice workflows.
4. Judicial inconsistency in Religious Courts between progressive *ex officio* protection and formalistic *petitum* adherence, plus weak *nafkah anak* enforcement.

## 8. Ex-Post RIA Report (Dimensions A–J)
Structured for IJRIO interoperability: strong normative alignment with CEDAW/UU 7/1984, constrained by resource, interpreter, and post-judgment enforcement gaps.

## 9. Meaningful Participation Assessment
Requires trauma-informed, accessible consultation channels (`MPI-02`, `MPI-03`) under explicit `GATE-03` approval.

## 10. Policy Recommendation Matrix (Draft — `GATE-05` Gated)
- Expand dedicated budget lines for disability interpreters and psychological accompaniment in courts.
- Standardise *ex officio* protection and child-maintenance enforcement tracking across Religious and General Courts.
