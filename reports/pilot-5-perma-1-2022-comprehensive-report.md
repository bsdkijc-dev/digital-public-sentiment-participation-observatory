# Pilot 5 Comprehensive Research Report: PERMA No. 1 Tahun 2022

> **Status:** `LIVE_PILOT_5_ACTIVE` (Level 1–2 Institutional Baseline Verified; strict UU PDP 27/2022 & SK KMA 2-144/2022 victim-privacy protection; not an official Mahkamah Agung RI publication).

## 1. Research Background
Evaluates procedural access, LPSK coordination, and execution barriers for crime-victim restitution and state compensation under PERMA No. 1 Tahun 2022.

## 2. Regulatory Profile
- **Primary Instrument**: [PERMA No. 1 Tahun 2022 tentang Tata Cara Penyelesaian Permohonan dan Pemberian Restitusi dan Kompensasi kepada Korban Tindak Pidana](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/209702/perma-no-1-tahun-2022) (effective 1 March 2022, BN.2022/No.225, 33 pages).
- **Key Procedural Rules** ([Dandapala / BSDK MA RI](https://dandapala.com/article/detail/hakim-tinggi-heru-wibowo-kupas-tuntas-restitusi-dan-kompensasi)): Petitions must be decided by *penetapan* within **21 days** of the first hearing (final *banding* appeal). Covers restitution (offender-paid) and state compensation (gross human rights violations, terrorism, and sexual violence under Pasal 35 UU TPKS).

## 3. Public Issue Map
- `TAX-05` (Vulnerable Group & Gender Protection): Trauma-informed victim notification and legal aid access.
- `TAX-06` (Victim Restitution & Enforcement): LPSK calculation workflows, offender insolvency, and asset seizure/auction.

## 4. Discourse Analysis & Ethics Guardrail
Under **UU No. 27 Tahun 2022** and **SK KMA 2-144/2022**, Pilot 5 prohibits scraping or storing identifiable victim narratives and relies strictly on Level 1–2 institutional evidence until consent-gated `GATE-03` consultations are approved.

## 5. Stakeholder Perspective Report
Covers crime victims, LPSK, prosecutors (*penuntut umum*), investigators, criminal court judges, and victim-support CSOs.

## 6. Evidence Validation Report (Verified Level 1–2 Findings)
Logged in `validated-evidence/pilot5_live_official_records.csv`:
- **2026 Post-Inkracht Precedent**: PN Manado Determination `1/Res-Pid/2026/PN Mnd` (25 June 2026, [PN Manado](https://pn-manado.go.id/pn-manado-tetapkan-restitusi-korban-kekerasan-seksual-pasca-putusan-inkrah/)) awarded Rp17,514,000 restitution post-final judgment via LPSK.
- **Post-KUHAP Baru Analysis**: [Dandapala (Oct 2026)](https://dandapala.com/opini/detail/efektivitas-perma-12022-terhadap-hak-restitusi-korban-tppo-pasca-kuhap-baru) and [Jurnal ULB Advokasi (2025)](https://jurnal.ulb.ac.id/index.php/advokasi/article/viewFile/7847/5191) examine TPPO and sexual-violence restitution gaps.

## 7. Implementation Gap Analysis
1. Low victim awareness of restitution rights and uneven proactive notification by investigators/prosecutors.
2. Offender inability or refusal to pay ordered restitution, with underutilised asset seizure/auction mechanisms.
3. Need for procedural harmonisation between PERMA 1/2022 and KUHAP Baru (UU No. 20 Tahun 2025).

## 8. Ex-Post RIA Report (Dimensions A–J)
Structured for IJRIO interoperability: strong 21-day adjudication design offset by post-order collection and cross-agency notification bottlenecks.

## 9. Meaningful Participation Assessment
Requires victim-safe, anonymised consultation channels (`MPI-02`, `MPI-03`) under explicit `GATE-03` approval.

## 10. Policy Recommendation Matrix (Draft — `GATE-05` Gated)
- Harmonise PERMA 1/2022 with KUHAP Baru and standardise mandatory judicial/prosecutorial notification of victim restitution rights.
- Strengthen asset-tracing and execution coordination between prosecutors, courts, and LPSK.
