## 3. Cross-Cutting Theme: Adjudication Speed vs. Post-Judgment Enforcement
Across Pilots 1, 2, 4, and 5, Indonesia's judiciary demonstrates strong first-instance procedural clearance (e.g., **92.63%** in *gugatan sederhana*, **44.72%** mediation resolution), while public and institutional friction concentrates in **post-judgment execution** (*eksekusi putusan*, *nafkah anak*, and criminal *restitusi*).
