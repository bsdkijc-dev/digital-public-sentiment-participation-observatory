# Pilot 1 Regulatory Profile: PERMA No. 1 Tahun 2016

- **Official Title**: Peraturan Mahkamah Agung Nomor 1 Tahun 2016 tentang Prosedur Mediasi di Pengadilan
- **Official Source**: [JDIH BPK](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/209641/perma-no-1-tahun-2016) (BN.2016/No.175, 30 pages)
- **Enactment / Effective Date**: Enacted 3 February 2016; effective 4 February 2016
- **Legal Status**: **IN FORCE** (revoked PERMA No. 01 Tahun 2008)
- **Electronic Complement**: **PERMA No. 3 Tahun 2022** tentang Mediasi di Pengadilan Secara Elektronik ([JDIH BPK PDF](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/211652/Perma%20Nomor%203%20Tahun%202022.pdf?embed=True)), whose Pasal 25 preserves PERMA No. 1 Tahun 2016 for electronic mediation unless otherwise regulated.
- **Key Focus Articles for DSPPO Pilot 1**:
  - **Pasal 4**: Mandatory mediation scope and exceptions.
  - **Pasal 7**: Good-faith obligation (*itikad baik*) and sanctions for non-attendance.
  - **Pasal 13**: Certified judge and non-judge mediator requirements.
  - **Pasal 17 & Pasal 24**: Mediation timeline (30 days, extendable) and settlement (*akta perdamaian*).
