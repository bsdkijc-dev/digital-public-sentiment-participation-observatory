## Pilot Selection Decision
**PERMA No. 1 Tahun 2016 (Prosedur Mediasi di Pengadilan)** is confirmed as the **Initial Research Pilot**, evaluated alongside its electronic mediation complement (**PERMA No. 3 Tahun 2022**).
