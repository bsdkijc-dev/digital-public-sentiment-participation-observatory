# Policy Brief (Draft): Strengthening Court-Annexed Mediation (PERMA No. 1 Tahun 2016)

> **Human Approval Gate (`GATE-05`):** Draft advisory brief only. Requires explicit human approval before external publication or institutional submission.

## Executive Summary
Preliminary Pilot 1 scoping under DSPPO v1.1 identifies three priority areas where public feedback and implementation evidence intersect for [PERMA No. 1 Tahun 2016](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/209641/perma-no-1-tahun-2016) and [PERMA No. 3 Tahun 2022](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/211652/Perma%20Nomor%203%20Tahun%202022.pdf?embed=True):
1. **Good-Faith Enforcement (Pasal 7)**: Standardise judicial and mediator checklists to prevent pro-forma mediation attendance.
2. **Certified Mediator Distribution (Pasal 13)**: Address rural availability gaps and clarify non-judge mediator fee structures upfront.
3. **Accessible Electronic Mediation (Pasal 4 PERMA 3/2022)**: Provide plain-language pre-mediation guides and low-bandwidth contingency protocols.
