# IJRIO Ex-Post RIA Traceability Chain

DSPPO connects public discourse and stakeholder participation to **IJRIO** (`bsdkijc-dev/indonesia-judicial-regulatory-impact-observatory`) through a mandatory 7-step traceability chain:

`PUBLIC CONCERN → RESEARCH QUESTION → VALIDATED EVIDENCE → REGULATORY IMPACT FINDING → POLICY RECOMMENDATION → OFFICIAL RESPONSE → FUTURE REGULATORY IMPROVEMENT`

## Mandatory Evaluation Dimensions (A–J)
For every selected PERMA (starting with `PERMA No. 1 Tahun 2016`), DSPPO + IJRIO evaluate:
- **A.** Regulatory identification (`perma_registry.csv`)
- **B.** Regulatory objectives
- **C.** Historical policy problem
- **D.** Public concerns (`public_issue_registry.csv`)
- **E.** Stakeholder experiences (`consultation_submissions.csv`)
- **F.** Implementation evidence (`validated_evidence.csv`, `implementation_gap_analysis.csv`)
- **G.** Access-to-justice impact (`judicial_access_indicators.csv`)
- **H.** Institutional efficiency
- **I.** Unintended consequences (`regulatory_impact_evidence.csv`)
- **J.** Regulatory reform alternatives (`public_recommendations.csv`, `policy_response_matrix.csv`)

> **Causal Guardrail:** Public discourse never proves causal regulatory impact on its own; causal claims require formal ex-post RIA triangulation.
