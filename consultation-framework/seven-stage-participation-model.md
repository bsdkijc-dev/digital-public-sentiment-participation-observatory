# Seven-Stage Public Participation Framework

DSPPO distinguishes passive social listening from **meaningful public participation** using a seven-stage lifecycle:

1. **STAGE 1 — LISTEN**: Identify public concerns through lawful, proportionate social listening (official portals, public app reviews, API-compliant broadcasts).
2. **STAGE 2 — IDENTIFY**: Translate recurring concerns into clearly defined, testable policy problems mapped to specific PERMA articles.
3. **STAGE 3 — VALIDATE**: Compare discourse findings against Level 1–2 evidence (MA RI Laptah, IKM, judicial statistics, LBH/academic studies).
4. **STAGE 4 — INVITE**: Provide accessible, opt-in opportunities for affected communities (litigants, mediators, legal aid, court users) to contribute.
5. **STAGE 5 — CONSIDER**: Document how researchers and policymakers assess substantive contributions without cherry-picking.
6. **STAGE 6 — RESPOND**: Prepare `policy_response_matrix.csv` classifying each recommendation as `ACCEPTED`, `PARTIALLY_ACCEPTED`, or `REJECTED` with explicit reasons.
7. **STAGE 7 — EVALUATE**: Determine whether contributions influenced regulatory design, implementation guidance, or future PERMA reform.

> **Core Rule:** Measure participation quality, not merely participant count. A reasoned rejection demonstrates genuine consideration; a high acceptance rate alone does not prove meaningful participation.
