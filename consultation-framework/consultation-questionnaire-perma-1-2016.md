# Stage 4 Consultation Instrument — PERMA No. 1 Tahun 2016 (DRAFT)

> **Human Approval Gate (`GATE-03`):** This consultation instrument is a **draft** and must NOT be deployed externally without explicit human approval.

## Consent & UU PDP Notice
Participation is voluntary and anonymous. Do not submit names, NIKs, case numbers, or personal contact details. Responses are used solely for public-interest research under [UU No. 27 Tahun 2022](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/229798/uu-no-27-tahun-2022).

## Core Questions (Bilingual ID / EN)
1. **Role / Peran**: Litigant (*Pihak Berperkara*), Certified Mediator (*Mediator*), Advocate/LBH (*Advokat/Pendamping*), or Court Staff (*Aparatur Pengadilan*).
2. **Clarity & Cost (`TAX-01`)**: Were mediation procedures and potential non-judge mediator fees explained clearly before mediation began?
3. **Good-Faith Compliance (`TAX-03`, Pasal 7)**: Did all principals attend in good faith, and how effectively were Pasal 7 rules applied?
4. **Electronic Mediation (`TAX-02`, PERMA 3/2022)**: If conducted online, what technical or accessibility barriers occurred?
5. **Reform Suggestion**: What concrete procedural or guidance change would improve mediation fairness and settlement quality?
