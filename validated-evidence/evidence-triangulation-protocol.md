# Evidence Validation and Triangulation Protocol (Phase 6)

No Level 3 public discourse finding may be treated as verified empirical fact until triangulated against Level 1 (Official) or Level 2 (Institutional/Academic) evidence.

## Triangulation Classification
- **`CORROBORATED`**: Discourse issue aligns with Level 1 statistics ([MA RI Laptah 2024–2025](https://kepaniteraan.mahkamahagung.go.id/id/publikasi/laporan-tahunan), [Bawas IKM](https://bawas.mahkamahagung.go.id/blog/read/hasil-survei-indeks-kepuasan-masyarakat)) or peer-reviewed Level 2 research.
- **`PARTIAL_MATCH`**: Discourse reflects a real operational friction, but scope or frequency differs from official aggregate records.
- **`UNCORROBORATED`**: Recurring public claim lacking independent administrative or empirical verification; logged for targeted Phase 7–8 inquiry.
- **`CONFLICTING`**: Public discourse contradicts official statistics; both are preserved with provenance and methodological limitations.
