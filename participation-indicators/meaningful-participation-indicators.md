# Meaningful Participation Indicators

Grounded in three procedural rights: **Right to be Heard**, **Right to be Considered**, and **Right to Receive an Explanation**.

| Indicator ID | Indicator Name | Operational Definition | Data Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `MPI-01` | **Stakeholder Diversity** | Share of distinct affected groups represented (litigants, mediators, advocates, CSOs, court staff) | `stakeholder_groups.csv` |
| `MPI-02` | **Accessibility of Consultation** | Availability of bilingual/plain-language, low-bandwidth, and non-digital submission channels | `consultation_submissions.csv` |
| `MPI-03` | **Representation of Affected Communities** | Proportion of submissions from directly affected or marginalised groups (e.g., rural, women, UMKM) | `consultation_submissions.csv` |
| `MPI-04` | **Evidence Quality** | Share of submissions backed by verifiable experiential, procedural, or empirical evidence | `validated_evidence.csv` |
| `MPI-05` | **Substantive Consideration Rate** | Percentage of unique recommendations logged with documented analytical assessment | `policy_response_matrix.csv` |
| `MPI-06` | **Documented Response Rate** | Percentage of recommendations receiving a written `ACCEPTED`/`PARTIALLY_ACCEPTED`/`REJECTED` rationale | `policy_response_matrix.csv` |
| `MPI-07` | **Policy Traceability** | End-to-end linkage from `PUBLIC CONCERN` to `REGULATORY IMPACT FINDING` and `RECOMMENDATION` | `regulatory_impact_evidence.csv` |
| `MPI-08` | **Post-Enactment Feedback** | Presence of structured post-implementation monitoring loops after PERMA revision | `research_quality_log.csv` |
