# Research Source Hierarchy (DSPPO v1.1)

1. **LEVEL 1 — Official Sources**: JDIH Mahkamah Agung RI, JDIH BPK (`peraturan.bpk.go.id`), official PERMA publications, Mahkamah Agung Annual Reports (`Laptah`), official judicial statistics, and government open-data portals (`lapor.go.id`, `siwas.mahkamahagung.go.id`).
2. **LEVEL 2 — Institutional Evidence**: Judicial research publications (BSDK MA RI), legal aid organisation reports (LBH/YLBHI), professional association publications (PERADI, IKAHI), university research, and public institutional consultations.
3. **LEVEL 3 — Public Discourse**: Publicly accessible social-media posts (API-compliant), public discussion forums, online news discussions, and public comments on institutional announcements. *Never treated as verified factual evidence without Level 1–2 triangulation.*
4. **LEVEL 4 — Comparative Evidence**: OECD, Estonia e-Governance Academy, European Commission (CEPEJ), World Justice Project, World Bank, and peer-reviewed comparative research.

Every record must log provenance, publication date, retrieval date, URL, collection methodology, and limitations.
