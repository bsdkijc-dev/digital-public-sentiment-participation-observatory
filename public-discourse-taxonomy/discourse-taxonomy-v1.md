## Sentiment Classification Scale
1. `POSITIVE` — Constructive endorsement or reported successful resolution (not equated with policy success).
2. `NEGATIVE` — Reported barrier, dissatisfaction, or procedural failure (not equated with institutional failure).
3. `NEUTRAL` — Informational, procedural inquiry, or descriptive discussion.
4. `MIXED` — Co-occurring positive and negative elements (e.g., faster filing but poor connectivity).
5. `UNCLEAR` — Ambiguous, sarcastic, or insufficient context for reliable coding.
